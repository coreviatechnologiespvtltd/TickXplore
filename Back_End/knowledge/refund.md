# Refunds on TickXplore

If your plans change, you can request a refund on an eligible booking. Every refund
request is reviewed by the TickXplore admin team and you receive email updates at
each step.

## How to use the refund feature

Go to your dashboard and open Booking History. For upcoming bookings you can request
a refund by providing a reason. The admin will review the request and notify you via
email once it has been approved or declined.

## How to request a refund step by step

1. Sign in and open the Refunds or Booking History page.
2. Choose an upcoming booking that is eligible for cancellation.
3. Select the booking and ask for a refund.
4. Give a reason for the refund and submit the request.

## How long does a refund take

Once you submit a refund request the admin reviews it and you receive a status
update. After approval the refund is processed and you are informed by email at each
step. TickXplore does not publish a fixed turnaround time, so the time depends on when
your request is reviewed.

## Checking the status of your refund

You can see the status of a refund from your booking history or the Refunds page, and
you will also be emailed when the request is approved or declined. If you are signed
in, you can also ask the TickXplore assistant to check the refund status of one of
your bookings.

## Important refund notes

- A refund has to be requested for an eligible upcoming booking.
- You need to give a reason when you request a refund.
- You can only have one pending refund request per booking, so wait for a decision
  before submitting another.
- Once a refund is approved the booking is cancelled and TickXplore informs the
  vendor.
