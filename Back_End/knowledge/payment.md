# Payment methods on TickXplore

TickXplore supports two payment methods: secure online payment through Khalti, and
Cash on Visit for in-person payment at the time of travel.

## What payment options are available

TickXplore supports secure online payments through Khalti as well as Cash on Visit
(CoV), where you pay the vendor directly at the time of travel.

## Paying online with Khalti

When you pay online you choose Khalti at the payment step and complete the payment
on the Khalti checkout page. Once the payment is confirmed your booking is marked as
Booked and the payment status becomes Paid. Your ticket is then sent to you by email.

## How Cash on Visit works

When booking a bus seat or a vehicle you can choose Cash on Visit as your payment
method. Your booking is reserved straight away and you pay the vendor in person at
the time of travel. The platform handles the confirmation, so you just show up with
your booking details. No money is taken from you during the booking itself.

## Cash on Visit bookings

Because the seats are reserved for you when you choose Cash on Visit, please make
sure you travel with your booking details. A Cash on Visit booking is confirmed
separately, and you will receive notifications about the booking confirmation and
status updates.

## Checking the payment status of a booking

Open My Bookings or Booking History to see each booking with its payment status. A
booking shows as Paid once an online payment is completed, and as Cash on Visit when
you have chosen to pay the vendor at the time of travel. If you are unsure about a
specific booking, you can ask the TickXplore assistant to look it up for you while
you are signed in.

## Discounts

TickXplore occasionally offers discounts and special offers on certain routes and
vehicles. Keep an eye on the homepage and announcements for the latest deals.
