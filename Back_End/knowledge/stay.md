# Stays on TickXplore — hotels and villas

Alongside bus tickets and vehicle reservations, TickXplore lets you discover and
search places to stay. This is the Stay feature, reached from the Stay page of the
website.

## What the Stay feature covers

The Stay page lets you search accommodation across Nepal and compare places to stay
before or during your journey. You can search by:

- City or area, so you can find stays in the destination you are travelling to.
- Accommodation type, including Hotel, Villa, Apartment, Guest House, Private Room,
  Resort, Lodge and Homestay.
- The number of guests you are travelling with.
- Your price range.
- Amenities you need, such as Wi-Fi, parking or breakfast.
- Star rating.
- Your dates, so you can see what is available for your stay.

## Hotels and villas

Hotels and villas are both available on TickXplore. A villa is one of the
accommodation types you can filter by, alongside hotels, apartments, guest houses,
private rooms, resorts, lodges and homestays. You can open a listing to see its
photographs, description, amenities, room types and policies.

## Seeing prices and availability for a stay

When you search with your check-in and check-out dates, TickXplore shows the nightly
prices and whether the property has rooms free for those dates. Each listing shows its
policies, such as check-in and check-out times, minimum and maximum stay length, and
its cancellation policy, so you can check them before you decide.

## Booking a room on TickXplore

Room booking and reservation for a stay is not available on TickXplore yet. The Stay
feature currently lets you search, browse listings, and check prices and availability,
but you cannot complete a room booking or reservation through TickXplore at this time.
The assistant cannot book a room or a villa either.

## For hotel owners

If you own a hotel, a villa or another accommodation, you can register on TickXplore
and manage your listing, room types, prices, photographs, availability and policies
from the hotel dashboard. A listing is only visible to travellers once it has been
approved by the TickXplore admin team.
