# Vendors on TickXplore

Vendors are the transport operators who list buses and vehicles on TickXplore and
receive bookings for them. This page covers how to become a vendor and what the vendor
dashboard lets you do.

## How to register as a vendor

Use the vendor registration option on the sign-up page. Fill in your business and
vehicle details and submit the form. Vendors only become active after an admin
activates their account, at which point you can start managing bookings. Your vendor
application is reviewed by the TickXplore admin team before you can sign in to the
vendor dashboard.

## How the vendor dashboard works

Once you are approved and signed in as a vendor, you can access the vendor dashboard
to manage your vehicles or buses, upload images, update availability, set prices, and
track booking stats and refunds. The dashboard shows the bookings made against the
buses and vehicles you have listed.

## Managing your buses and vehicles

From the vendor dashboard you can add the buses and vehicles you operate, set their
prices, upload photographs, and update their availability. Availability changes are
what travellers see when they search for a trip, so keeping it up to date matters.

## Sharing live locations

Vendors share the live location of their buses and vehicles during a trip. This is
what powers the live tracking your travellers see on the TickXplore dashboard. If a
vehicle's live location is not visible to a customer, it may simply not have been
broadcast yet — check again closer to the departure time.

## Earnings and commission

Bookings you receive through TickXplore show your earnings and TickXplore's
commission in the vendor dashboard, so you can track the bookings and refunds for your
listed transport.

## Account approval and status

A new vendor account is not active immediately. You will receive an email once the
admin team has reviewed your application. If your account is not yet active, sign-in
will ask you to wait for admin approval.
