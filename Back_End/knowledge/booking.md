# Booking a bus ticket on TickXplore

TickXplore is a transport booking platform in Nepal. You can book bus tickets, reserve
vehicles such as 4x4s, jeeps and Scorpios, and browse tourist destinations, all in one
place. This page explains how to book a ticket and how to find it afterwards.

## How to book a ticket

1. Go to the Tickets section of the website (the "Book Bus Tickets" page).
2. Choose your destination — you select buses from various routes and locations
   across Nepal, so you can board from the point that suits your journey.
3. Choose your travel date and the bus you want.
4. On the seat selection page, review the available seats and remaining availability
   for that bus, then select your seats. Seat selection is available for buses so
   your group can sit together.
5. Enter the passenger details for everyone travelling.
6. Choose how you want to pay: secure online payment through Khalti, or Cash on Visit.
7. Complete the payment. Your booking is confirmed only after your preferred seats
   are selected and the booking step is completed.

## Booking several seats or tickets at once

Yes. You can book multiple seats for your group during a single booking, and seat
selection is available for buses so your group can sit together.

## Viewing your bookings after you book

Log in to TickXplore and open the My Bookings page from your dashboard, or open
Booking History. Your bookings are listed with their status, price and refund
details. A completed online booking is shown as Booked with payment status Paid.

## Getting your ticket

When your booking is confirmed you receive a booking number and a ticketing email
containing your ticket. From My Bookings you can also ask for the ticket to be sent
to your email again.

## Seat availability

Availability changes as other travellers book, so always confirm on the seat
selection screen before paying. If a seat has been taken in the meantime you will be
told when the booking is checked and you can pick different seats.

## Tourist destinations and packages

TickXplore supports booking for tourist destinations or packages in select areas
under the Tourist Booking section. Popular destinations include Chitwan, Poon Hill,
Nagarkot, Ghandruk, Kalinchok, Langtang and Mustang. You can browse these in the
Tourist Areas section of the site.
