# Reserving a vehicle on TickXplore

TickXplore lets you reserve a whole vehicle for your trip instead of buying a single
seat. This is the practical option for groups, families and mountain routes.

## How to reserve a vehicle

1. Visit the Vehicle Booking section on TickXplore.
2. Select the vehicle you want, such as a jeep, 4x4, or Scorpio.
3. Pick your dates — you choose the period the vehicle is reserved for.
4. Enter your pickup and drop-off locations.
5. Proceed to payment to reserve the vehicle.

## Vehicle types you can reserve

You can reserve a variety of vehicles including 4x4s, jeeps, Scorpios, tourist buses
and e-vans. These are ideal for mountain trips and group tours, with pickup and
drop-off locations captured at booking.

## What a whole-vehicle reservation means

A vehicle reservation is for the vehicle, not for one seat. Once the vehicle is
reserved for your date range it is not available to other travellers for that period,
so availability depends on the dates you choose rather than on seat counts.

## Checking availability and live location

Each vehicle has a live location that the vendor shares during the trip. From your
user dashboard you can track your booked bus or vehicle in real time, so you always
know where your ride is. Live tracking updates are shared by the vendor, so if a
location is not yet available it may not have been broadcast yet — check back again
closer to your departure time.

## Vehicle bookings and payment

A vehicle reservation follows the same payment rules as a bus seat: you can pay online
through Khalti, or choose Cash on Visit and pay the vendor at the time of travel. A
vehicle booking is recorded on your booking history just like a bus ticket.
