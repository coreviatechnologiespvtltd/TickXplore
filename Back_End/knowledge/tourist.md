# Tourist destinations on TickXplore

Besides transport, TickXplore helps you plan where to go. You can browse tourist areas
across Nepal and book tourist destinations or packages in select areas.

## Which tourist areas can I book

Popular destinations such as Chitwan, Poon Hill, Nagarkot, Ghandruk, Kalinchok,
Langtang and Mustang are available under the Tourist Areas and booking sections in
select areas. The Tourist Areas page on the website lists the destinations TickXplore
currently covers.

## Can I book tourist entries

Yes. TickXplore supports booking for tourist destinations or packages in select areas
under the Tourist Booking section. Tourist destinations and packages are offered
separately from bus and vehicle transport, so you can arrange a destination visit as
well as your journey to it.

## Combining a trip with a destination

Most travellers use TickXplore to book the transport part of a journey — a bus seat or
a reserved vehicle — and then book a tourist destination or package. You can search
for buses and vehicles for your route and browse the tourist areas from the same
account.

## Tourist blogs

TickXplore also publishes travel articles for the destinations it covers. These are
available from the website and can help you plan what to do at a destination.

## Live tracking during your trip

If you have already booked, from your user dashboard you can track your booked bus or
vehicle in real time during your trip, so you always know where your ride is. Live
tracking updates are shared by the vendor, so a location may not be available
immediately — check back again closer to your departure time.
