# Your TickXplore account

This page covers creating an account, staying informed about your journeys, and
managing your password.

## How do I create an account

Visit the registration page, fill in your details, and choose whether you are
registering as a user, vendor, or hotel. Verification is completed through OTP to
secure your account. Once your account is verified you can sign in and start booking.

## I forgot my password. How do I reset it

Use the forgot password option, request an OTP, and then choose a new password. You
can also change your password from your profile at any time.

## Do I get notifications about my bookings

Yes. You receive notifications for booking confirmations, status updates, refund
decisions, and other important activity related to your account and journeys. These
appear in your notifications on the site, and important decisions such as a refund
result are also sent to you by email.

## My profile

Your profile page holds your personal details, your current location, and your profile
photo. You can update these details at any time, and change your password from the
same page.

## Hotel partners

Hotels can register on TickXplore and manage their own listing, rooms, prices and
availability from the hotel dashboard. If you run a hotel and want to be listed, use
the hotel registration option when you sign up.
