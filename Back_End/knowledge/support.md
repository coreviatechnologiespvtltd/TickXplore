# Getting help with TickXplore

If this assistant cannot answer your question, or if something has gone wrong with a
booking, there are several ways to get help.

## How to contact support

Visit the FAQs section for answers to the most common questions, or scroll to the
bottom of the website to find the contact details. You can also get help through the
TickXplore chatbot, which can look up your own bookings, payment status and refund
status while you are signed in.

## Where to find your own booking details

Most questions are answered by looking at your own account rather than contacting
anyone. Sign in and open:

- My Bookings — your current and upcoming bookings.
- Booking History — all past and upcoming bookings with their status, price and refund
  details.
- Refunds — refund requests and their decisions.
- Profile — your account details, and password changes.

## Using the TickXplore assistant

The assistant is available on every page of the site. Ask it how something works — for
example how to book a ticket, what payment methods are available, how Cash on Visit
works, or how refunds are reviewed. While you are signed in it can also look up your
own bookings, seat availability, payment status and refund status. It will always
confirm with you before it cancels or changes a booking.

## Common questions covered by the assistant

- How to book a ticket
- How to reserve a vehicle
- What payment methods are available
- How Cash on Visit works
- How refunds are reviewed and how long they take
- How to register as a vendor
- How to check the status of your booking

## Booking, history and refund pages

For anything specific to your account, the booking, history and refund pages in your
dashboard have the full details. The FAQs page covers general questions about
TickXplore.
