# Cancelling a booking on TickXplore

If you no longer need a trip you booked on TickXplore you can cancel it. Cancelling
frees your seats so another traveller can book them.

## How to cancel your booking

You can request a cancellation for eligible upcoming bookings from your booking
history. Not all bookings may be eligible, so check the booking status and conditions
before requesting. You will see your upcoming and past bookings, together with their
status, on the Booking History page of your dashboard.

## Cancelling from the TickXplore assistant

The assistant can find your booking and prepare a cancellation for you, but it always
asks you to confirm first. Nothing is cancelled until you confirm, so you are always
in control. You can also cancel directly from the My Bookings or Refunds page.

## What happens when a booking is cancelled

The booking is marked as Cancelled and the reserved seats on the bus are released so
other travellers can book them. You receive a notification confirming the
cancellation. Cancelling a booking is not the same as receiving a refund — if you
want your money back you also need to request a refund, which the admin team reviews
separately.

## Cancelling and refunds together

Usually you cancel a booking and then request a refund for it. The refund request is
reviewed by the admin team and you are notified by email once a decision is made.
See the refund page for how the refund process works.

## Vehicle bookings

A vehicle reservation is for the whole vehicle rather than individual seats. If you
need to cancel a vehicle booking, please do it from the My Bookings or Refunds page so
the vehicle and its availability are released correctly for other travellers.
